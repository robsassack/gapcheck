GapCheck compares the requirements in a job posting with a resume saved locally in Chrome. Select the relevant job-description text, open GapCheck’s side panel, and run an on-device analysis.

Features:
- Paste a resume or import a text-based PDF.
- Review a match score and concise summary.
- See covered requirements, partial matches, and gaps with supporting resume evidence.
- Process your resume and selected job text locally using Chrome’s built-in AI.

GapCheck does not automatically read entire webpages, monitor browsing activity, or send your resume and selected job text to the developer. Analysis is informational and does not guarantee hiring outcomes.

Requires Chrome 138 or later on a supported desktop device. Chrome may download its built-in AI model the first time analysis runs.
